Lippe Optics is a new start-up based in Lippstadt, Germany, specializing in laser technologies. 
Our focus is the development of advanced laser targeting systems for defense applications

Lippe Optics’ mission is to develop cutting-edge laser targeting systems for defense
applications. Our systems aim to be reliable, precise, eco-friendly and easy to set up,
addressing current costumer needs. Our first product is the Mosquito Laser Targeting System,
designed to accurately shoot down mosquitos using lasers that are safe to both humans and
the environment.

Keys to Success
The demand for innovative and precise mosquito control solutions, especially for outdoor
events, is rapidly increasing. Lippe Optics can succeed in this market by focusing on several
key factors:
1. Secure component supply & manufacturing: With proper planning, contract
manufacturing of the system is possible. To achieve this, a reliable stock of the main
components (laser modules, sensor, cameras) must be maintained.
2. Targeted sales: Interest in our laser technology may come from various industries, but
our primary focus will remain on our target market segments: event organizers,
municipalities, and institutions that require scalable solutions from 10 to over 1,000
units.
3. Build strong brand recognition: It is important to rapidly establish brand recognition for
precision, safety, and innovation. The potential for this market will attract competitors, so
early branding and visibility are crucial for the company’s success.

2.0 Products
Lippe Optics’ current product is a mosquito defense system based around several dual-axis
mounted laser turrets that use advanced sensors and high precision targeting systems to
shoot mosquitos out of the air using lasers.
2.1 Product Description
Our first product is called the “Mosquito Laser Targeting System”, a temporary title as the
product is still in the prototype stage. This system consists of a turret that holds the laser
“shooting” module. The purpose of the product is to detect and eliminate mosquitos by
using a combination of lasers, sensors, and cameras, all programmed to work seamlessly
together to achieve precise targeting.
The turret enables 360° rotation, allowing the system to track and target mosquitos from
any direction. Designed with portability in mind, the device is compact and lightweight,
measuring approximately 2.5 cm in height and 20 cm in length. This slim profile makes it
easy to mount, relocate, or integrate into existing setups such as event spaces or outdoor
venues.
Power is supplied by an 11.1V rechargeable battery pack, enabling extended use while
maintaining portability. Additionally, plans are underway to integrate a small solar panel to
recharge the battery for longer outdoor operation and increased energy efficiency.
Looking ahead, upon successful initial sales, we plan to enhance the system by offering
customization options such as different color schemes, add-on modules, and upgrades to
more powerful lasers and advanced camera systems, allowing the product to adapt to
varying customer needs.

3.0 Strategy and Implementation Summary
Lippe Optics will initially target the sector of pest control, focusing on outdoor event organizers
in Southern Europe, regions that frequently struggle with mosquito related issues. This market
provides a cost-effective environment to test and refine our laser targeting system.
Once we establish reliability and brand awareness in this market, we plan to gradually expand
into the defense sector, where our core laser targeting technology can be adapted into larger
scale systems. This phased approach allows us to gain market experience, validate the
product, and build credibility before entering the highly regulated defense industry.
3.1 Competitive Edge
Lippe Optics’ main competitive edge lies in its innovative use of laser targeting technology
for the elimination of mosquitos. Our systems combine precision, safety, and
eco-friendlines, features rarely found together in pest control solutions. Unlike chemical
repellents or traditional traps, our laser-based approach offers a clean, maintenance-free,
and targeted solution. Our team’s engineering background gives us flexibility and speed,
allowing us to adapt quickly to customer needs and market changes.
3.2 Marketing Strategy
Lippe Optics will begin by targeting outdoor event organizers and institutions in Southern
Europe, where mosquito problems are common. Our marketing will emphasize the
eco-friendly, precise, and chemical-free nature of our laser solution. We aim to build brand
awareness through partnerships with local events, universities, and technology expositions.
After proving the system in real-world conditions, we plan to expand into the high-value
defense sector, adapting our core laser technology for broader applications.
3.3 Sales Strategy
Lippe Optics will adopt a direct sales approach in the early stages, targeting event organizers
and institutions through email outreach and presence at local trade fairs. Initial sales will focus
