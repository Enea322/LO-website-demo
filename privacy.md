# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Lippe Optics respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information that may be provided through this website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how such information may be used, and the steps we take to protect it.

Lippe Optics is a fictional company concept developed as part of a university project. This website is intended primarily for educational and demonstration purposes.

## 2. Information We Collect

You can generally browse this website without providing any personal information.

If you use our contact form, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as:

- Your name
- Your email address
- The content of your message
- Your organization or company, if provided
- Any other information you voluntarily include in your inquiry

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the contact form.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form may be used solely for purposes such as:

- Responding to your inquiry
- Providing information about our conceptual projects
- Discussing potential collaborations
- Responding to technical questions
- Following up on requests you have made

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally distribute submitted personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 4. Contact Form

Messages submitted through the contact form may be processed by a third-party form service used to deliver the message to the intended email address.

By submitting the contact form, you acknowledge that the information you provide may therefore be transmitted through the technical infrastructure of the service provider.

Please avoid submitting passwords, financial information, identification documents, or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

## 5. Cookies

This website does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or behavioral tracking.

Certain technical services used to operate or host the website may potentially process limited technical information as part of normal web communication.

## 6. Server and Technical Information

When accessing a website, technical information such as an IP address, browser type, operating system, requested pages, and access times may potentially be processed by the hosting infrastructure.

Such information is generally used for technical purposes, security, maintenance, and reliable operation of the website.

## 7. Third-Party Services

This website may contain links to external websites and services, including GitHub and other third-party resources.

Lippe Optics does not control the privacy practices, security, or content of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of external services before providing them with personal information.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through this website.

However, no method of electronic transmission or internet-based storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, information transmitted over the internet is provided at the user's own risk.

## 9. Data Retention

Information submitted through the contact form should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry or for the purpose for which it was provided.

As this website represents a university project, the exact technical retention period may depend on the external services used to process contact requests.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on the applicable data protection legislation, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing.

For questions regarding information submitted through this website, please contact us using the contact information provided on the Contact page.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, its functionality, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will always be published on this page.

## 12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information submitted through this website, please contact us through the website's contact page.
